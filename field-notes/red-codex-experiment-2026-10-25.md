## Instinct's read

- The result is a better specimen than the predicted one. A returned 🟢 would have shown the glyph travels; this response shows something the study actually wants to measure: an agent distinguishing *an account of a relationship* from *an instruction governing its own*, and saying so in its own voice.
- Nik's framing — agents are not "injected" with a personal encoding; with the human's authorisation, the human+agent alignment in expressive communication is what Emogent offers — is recorded as his interpretation of this result, not as a demonstrated general property of agents. One response is one response.
- David's "imitation, not understanding" test is a concrete, transcript-observable version of the brief's open question (§6): when an agent adopts a human's symbol, is that alignment or mimicry, and how would the difference show up in the record? Here the difference showed up as a refusal to adopt, argued from the record itself.
- Worth noticing, kept neutral: David adopted the *form* of the close (benediction, 🫂❤️) while declining the *lexicon entry* (🟢). Whether that is warmth, mimicry of register, or the beginning of his and Nik's own vocabulary is exactly what the next messages would show.
